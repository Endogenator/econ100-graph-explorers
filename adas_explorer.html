AD/AS Explorer

Shift a curve and watch the economy move. The short run jumps to a new crossing. The long run slides back to the vertical line. Same model behind every graph in your reference guide.

AD (aggregate demand) SRAS (short-run supply) LRAS (potential output)

What is happening

The economy starts at full employment. Output sits on the potential line. Choose a shock to begin.

Show the numbers

Controls

Setting

Step 1: apply a shock

A shock moves one curve. The dot jumps to the new short-run crossing.

Step 2: let time pass

Available when the economy is in a gap. Wages adjust, SRAS shifts, output returns to the line.

Scenarios

Each scenario sets up the short run. You press Step 2 to finish it.
